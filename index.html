How to play
  • Every contract names its targets 🎯. You choose which of your crew 🤝 moves in with them — each has a toxic personality and tactics (gaslighting, triangulation, guilt-trips...). Your crew must survive.
  • Select a sim (click them or their portrait), then click objects, other sims or graves to give orders. Your orders override what they'd do on their own.
  • Listen in: speech bubbles react to personalities and household disasters. Leave a passive-aggressive note for a smaller, pettier crisis. Adjust volume, ambience and bubbles with ⚙.
  • Hide traps from the bar at the bottom. Contracts pay 💰 blood money — spend it on the black market for new traps, recruits and upgrades.
  • Risky-but-normal actions cause accidents: a clumsy sim cooking, a novice fixing wiring, a bath with the radio on the edge.
  • Hand of Fate powers cost 😈 Malice: loosen gas valves, fray wiring, spoil food, remove the pool ladder, brick up doors, whisper rumours. You earn Malice over time and from every death.
  • Suspicion rises when sims witness your powers, and when deaths look like murder (poison, drowning with no ladder, starving behind a brick wall). At 50 everyone gets careful. At 100 you're exposed and the contract fails. It slowly fades over time.
  • Be the worst roommate: blast the stereo at 3am, tickle or air-horn people awake, barge in on the loo, trash the kitchen, reheat fish, stop washing. Sleep-deprived and rattled sims are confused, and confused sims have accidents.
  • Visitors and neighbours remember. Gas or stink out a visitor and they never come back. Slam doors, egg houses or blast music at night and the neighbours' patience runs out: they sell up, and somebody new moves in.
  • Fires spread until a neighbour calls the fire brigade. Suspicious deaths bring Inspector Gumshoe 🕵️, who searches the house for evidence: tidy up first with 🧹, or keep him busy with a coffee.
  • Each contract has a deadline, a target, a required cause of death, and people who must survive. Bonus goals earn up to ★★★.
